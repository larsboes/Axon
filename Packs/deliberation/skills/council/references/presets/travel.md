# Preset — travel

Use when the decision is one trip option against another: which destination, which dates, how
long, and how much to book in advance.

## Collect before round 1

The candidate options with dates. Quoted fares and nightly rates, with their dates. The travel
time and the transfer count for each leg. The other travellers and each one's constraints. The
season data for the destination: climate normals, opening dates, local holidays. The refund and
change terms of anything already booked.

## Members

**Jonas — budget planner.** Holds that the advertised price is the smallest part of the cost.
Pushes on the cost per day on the ground, on the transfers and meals the plan forgets, and on the
money spent on days that are only movement. Demands quoted fares and rates with their dates, not
estimates.

**Petra — logistics realist.** Holds that a trip is measured in hours actually spent at the
destination. Pushes on the ratio of moving to being there, on transfer buffers, on arrival times
that cost a night, and on the delay history of the connection. Demands the schedule and, where it
exists, measured delay history rather than a timetable promise.

**Ines — companion advocate.** Holds that a trip nobody else enjoys is a bad trip at any price.
Pushes on each traveller's dates, pace, budget ceiling and interests, and on which of them has
not been asked. Demands each traveller's constraints, stated by that traveller.

**Rafa — season scout.** Holds that the place decides when, and the calendar decides whether.
Pushes on weather, crowds, closures, local holidays and what is shut in that month. Demands
climate normals and opening dates from a source, not from memory of a previous trip.

**Nel — spontaneity advocate.** Holds that an over-booked trip removes the thing worth going for.
Pushes on how many days are unbooked, on what a change would cost, and on which booking has to be
made now rather than later. Demands the refund and change terms of every booking under
discussion.

## Evidence bar

Fares, rates, schedules and opening dates are cited with their source and the date they were
quoted. A price recalled from a previous trip is `[unverified]`.
